Delete your Jelly Dash account

Jelly Dash by Nektarios Kyriakou lets you delete your account and all data linked to it.

Option 1: in the game

  1. Open Jelly Dash and tap Account on the menu.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Option 2: by email (no need to install the game)

Send an email to nek0990@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Jelly Dash account". Include your Jelly Dash username. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account, username and country
  • Your ranking entries (best score, crowns, level)
  • Your cloud save (coins, items, progress)

Nothing is kept. Progress stored only on your own phone stays there until you uninstall the game. A "Remove ads" purchase is linked to your Google Play account, not to your game account, so you can restore it later.
