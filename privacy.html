Jelly Dash · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Jelly Dash ("the game") is made by Nektarios Kyriakou. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. Contact: nek0990@gmail.com.

Playing as a guest

If you play as a guest, your username, country, coins, outfits and progress are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you sign in with Google, we use Google Firebase to create your game account. We store:

  • your Google account ID and email address (to sign you in),
  • the username and country you choose,
  • your game progress (coins, items, levels) so you can continue on another device,
  • your best score, crowns, coins, highest stage and level, which are shown publicly in the global rankings together with your username, country flag and the look of your jelly,
  • whether you are online right now (a green dot shown to other players),
  • your friends list and friend requests, and the challenges you send or receive. A challenge contains a recording of your run (your jelly's position over time) so the other player can race your "ghost".
  • live races: while you race a friend live, your jelly's position is shared with that friend in real time. Race data is deleted when the race ends.
  • if you allow notifications, a notification token for your phone (from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging), used only to tell you about friend requests, challenges and live race invites.

You can turn off friend requests and notifications, remove or block other players at any time. There is no chat: players cannot send each other messages, pictures or audio.

We do not show your email address to other players and we do not sell your data.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: one ad after a finished game that reached stage 2 (unless you bought "Remove ads"), and optional ads you choose to watch to revive. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and information about ad interactions. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. See how Google uses data from apps that show ads.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

In the game: Menu → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your username, rankings entries, profile, friends, challenges, notification tokens, cloud save and sign-in account. You can also request deletion here: delete your Jelly Dash account.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
